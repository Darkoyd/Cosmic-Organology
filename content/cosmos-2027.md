    text: |
      **Siete proyectos sobre las tensiones del cosmos para pensar la existencia.**

      Universidad de los Andes invited artists interested in working alongside
      space scientists. The projects are developed through artist-scientist
      partnerships and will be presented in an exhibition at Sala Colpatria
      in 2027. Many of the structures of the cosmos do not exist because one
      force overcomes another, but because different dynamics remain in
      tension: gravity and the expansion of the universe, pressure and
      collapse within a star, order and chaos. *Cósmico* takes this idea as
      its starting point and extends it to its working methodology: the
      tension between science and art, two fields often considered opposites
      yet driven by a shared impulse to question the world, becomes the
      driving force behind each project.

      The call sought collaborative investigations, not illustrations. Each
      partnership begins with a phenomenon or question drawn from space
      sciences and, through artistic practice, shifts it toward
      philosophical, ontological, and speculative inquiries, with the aim of
      generating a singular aesthetic experience. The resulting knowledge
      belongs neither exclusively to art nor to science, nor is it simply the
      sum of the two. The project includes partnerships with EAFIT, the 2027
      Bogotá Art Biennial, and the 2027 Image Festival, as well as a showcase
      of student collaborations and an exhibition of research posters on
      campus.
