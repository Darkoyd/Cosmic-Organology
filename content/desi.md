  - heading: What is DESI
    text: |
      The Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument, mounted on the Mayall
      Telescope at Kitt Peak, uses 5,000 robotic fiber positioners to take
      the spectra of tens of millions of galaxies and quasars, building the
      largest 3D map of the universe to date.

      Combined with other probes, DESI's measurements of baryon acoustic
      oscillations hint that dark energy may be evolving over time, rather
      than being the constant of the standard cosmological model.
      [desi.lbl.gov](https://www.desi.lbl.gov/)
  - heading: DESI Data
    text: |
      The project works with the public
      [Data Release 1](https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/releases/dr1/):
      redshift catalogs and spectra for millions of objects, from which the
      BAO scale is measured per tracer and epoch.
