lede: Organología de la Cartografía Cósmica. An art-science installation for COSMOS 2027.
sections:
  - style: statement
    text: |
      In 1965 two radio astronomers accidentally caught the echo of the
      early universe. That echo left a mark on how galaxies are arranged
      today: baryon acoustic oscillations, a sound wave fossilized in
      space. In 2025 the DESI collaboration used that fossil, measured
      across fifteen million galaxies, to report the strongest evidence
      yet that dark energy is changing over cosmic time.

      How does a sonic fossil that may be rewriting the fate of the
      universe feel, in the body?
  - cards:
      - title: Transduction
        shape: circle
        text: >
          Four cosmic epochs mapped by DESI (nearby galaxies, distant
          galaxies, quasars) become four sculptural organs: instruments
          that turn imperceptible physical quantities into sound,
          vibration and light.
      - title: Data
        shape: square
        text: >
          Public DESI DR1 catalogs, with DR2 forthcoming. BAO scale and
          expansion history, computed per tracer and epoch, become the
          working parameters of the piece.
      - title: Critical Organology
        shape: triangle
        text: >
          A line of practice that reads instruments, biological or
          technological, as cultural and epistemic mediators of
          perception, extended here to the acoustic fossil of the cosmos.
