sections:
  - style: statement
    text: |
      In 1965 two radio astronomers accidentally caught the echo of the
      early universe. That echo left a mark on how galaxies are arranged
      today: baryon acoustic oscillations, a sound wave fossilized in
      space. In 2025 the DESI collaboration used that fossil, measured
      across fifteen million galaxies, to report the strongest evidence
      yet that dark energy is changing over cosmic time.

      How does a sonic fossil that may be rewriting the fate of the
      universe feel, in the body?
  - heading: What is the project
    text: |
      *Cosmic Organology Cartography* is an interdisciplinary
      research-creation project that brings together astrophysics, design,
      and sculptural practice to explore new ways of perceiving the universe
      through the body.

      Drawing on scientific data from the international DESI (Dark Energy
      Spectroscopic Instrument) collaboration, the project investigates how
      imperceptible cosmological phenomena can be translated into sensory
      experiences. Its starting point is baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO):
      imprints of sound waves from the early universe that remain encoded in
      the distribution of galaxies and make it possible to study the history
      of the universe's expansion and the potential evolution of dark energy.

      Through a process of translation between scientific data and sensory
      experience, the project proposes the development of a hybrid
      sculptural-instrumental organ that transforms these measurements into
      relationships of sound, vibration, light, resonance, and materiality.

      Rather than representing or illustrating the cosmos, the installation
      seeks to construct an instrument of perception, a device that makes it
      possible to experience physically the phenomena that we would normally
      only access through scientific instruments, mathematical models, and
      astronomical data.
  - cards:
      - title: Transduction
        shape: circle
        text: >
          Four cosmic epochs mapped by DESI (nearby galaxies, distant
          galaxies, quasars) become four sculptural organs: instruments
          that turn imperceptible physical quantities into sound,
          vibration and light.
      - title: Data
        shape: square
        text: >
          Public DESI DR1 catalogs, with DR2 forthcoming. BAO scale and
          expansion history, computed per tracer and epoch, become the
          working parameters of the piece.
      - title: Critical Organology
        shape: triangle
        text: >
          A line of practice that reads instruments, biological or
          technological, as cultural and epistemic mediators of
          perception, extended here to the acoustic fossil of the cosmos.
  - heading: Ambitions
    items:
      - year: "01"
        title: Exploring new ways of perceiving the universe
        text: >
          Investigate how cosmological phenomena that radically exceed our
          perceptual capacities can acquire a sensory and bodily dimension,
          expanding the ways in which we engage with knowledge of the universe
          by exploring alternative ways of sensing, listening to, touching, and
          thinking about its phenomena.
      - year: "02"
        title: Developing a new sculptural-instrumental language
        text: >
          Create a hybrid sculptural organ capable of translating cosmological
          data into experiences of sound, vibration, light, resonance, and
          materiality. Sculpture functions not as a representation of a
          scientific phenomenon, but as an instrument that enables a sensory
          relationship with it.
      - year: "03"
        title: Investigating new forms of knowledge production between art and science
        text: >
          Develop a research-creation methodology in which astrophysics,
          design, and sculpture do not operate as independent disciplines, but
          as fields of knowledge that mutually transform one another. Creative
          practice becomes a tool for formulating questions, exploring
          possibilities, and producing new forms of knowledge.
      - year: "04"
        title: Bringing cosmological knowledge closer to new audiences
        text: >
          Generate experiences that allow both specialized and non-specialized
          audiences to engage critically with complex scientific data. The
          intention is not to simplify science, but to open up alternative ways
          of encountering it through embodied and sensory experience.
      - year: "05"
        title: Developing a transferable methodology for data-to-sensation transduction
        text: >
          Document and consolidate a protocol for exploring how scientific data
          can be transformed into perceptual experiences without losing its
          complexity, margins of uncertainty, or open-ended questions, as a
          starting point for future collaborations between art, design, and
          science.
